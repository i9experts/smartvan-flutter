Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SmartVan ("we", "our", "us") provides a school van tracking and management service. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number and profile photo.
  • Student and pickup details: student name, school, pickup and drop-off addresses.
  • Location data: real-time van location (from drivers) and, with your permission, your location for pickup points.
  • Payment records: fee status and payment history. We do not store card details.
  • Device information: device token for push notifications and basic diagnostic data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To show live van location and estimated arrival times.
  • To send notifications about pickups, drop-offs, delays and fees.
  • To manage accounts, routes, attendance and fee records.
  • To improve reliability, safety and support.

3. Sharing of Information

Your information is shared only with your child's assigned driver, your school or van operator, and service providers that help us run the app (such as hosting, maps and notifications). We do not sell your personal data.

4. Location Permissions

Location access is used only for the tracking features of the app. You can disable it at any time in your device settings, though some features may stop working.

5. Data Security

We use encryption in transit and access controls to protect your data. No system is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

6. Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

Accounts are created and managed by parents or guardians. We collect student information only for transport purposes.

8. Your Rights

You may access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

Questions? Email support@smartvan.pk.
