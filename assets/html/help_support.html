Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track the van?

Open the Home screen and tap the live map. The van appears once the driver starts the trip.

I am not getting notifications.

Make sure notifications are enabled in Profile settings and in your device settings, and that battery saver isn't restricting the app.

How do I reset my password?

Go to Profile → Change Password. If you're locked out, use "Forgot Password" on the login screen.

Where can I see my fee payments?

Go to Profile → Payment History.

How do I update my pickup location?

Contact your van operator or email us with the new address and we'll update it.
